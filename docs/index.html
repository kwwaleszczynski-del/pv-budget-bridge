PV budget bridge

How far was a PV plant from its P50 budget last month, and why? This page splits the gap into irradiance, temperature, degradation, soiling, snow, clipping, availability, curtailment, data gaps and an unexplained rest. It also tests PR and availability guarantees and checks the irradiance sensor the whole calculation depends on. Plants: three NREL PVDAQ rooftop systems in Las Vegas (2016-2018) and a synthetic 1.32 MWp plant in central Poland on ERA5 weather (2021-2024) whose every loss is known. Budgets: pvlib on the PVGIS typical year. Code and method.

Everything above leans on the plane-of-array sensor. It is compared with a clear-sky model, a satellite or reanalysis POA series and the plant itself. The checks look for a shifted clock, wrong orientation, shading at low sun, soiling and drift.
